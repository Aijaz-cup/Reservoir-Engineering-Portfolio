# Project 04 — Final Forecast Results

## Forecast design

- Forecast period: 1 October 2016 to 1 October 2021.
- Historical wells remain inactive during the forecast.
- No active water or gas injection occurs during the forecast.
- Each candidate is evaluated independently under a common 300 bar BHP producer control.
- BASE contains no active forecast wells.

## Engineering interpretation

**Candidate A** is the preferred technical screening target. It gives the largest five-year oil recovery and the highest initial oil productivity among the tested locations.

**Candidate C** remains a secondary opportunity. Its high remaining oil saturation produces meaningful oil, but the five-year recovery is lower than Candidate A and water cut remains high for much of the forecast.

**Candidate B** is rejected under the tested assumptions. Its response is overwhelmingly water dominated and provides very little oil recovery.

The experiment is a deterministic late-life standalone technical screening study. It does not represent a full active-field development optimization or a commercial drill/no-drill sanction decision.

High remaining oil saturation alone is not sufficient to identify the strongest infill target. Pore volume, permeability, pressure, sweep history, phase mobility, connectivity and completion design must be considered together.
